Guide sources

Each source becomes one guide at its own address on this server, for one Jellyfin guide provider limited to the tuners it's for. Jellyfin never contacts the sources itself: an XMLTV source is fetched at most every few minutes (General, below), Schedules Direct once a day.

Schedules Direct account

Shared by every Schedules Direct source. The plugin downloads only what changed, once a day (Scheduled Tasks → "Update Schedules Direct guide", at a time between 2:00 and 6:00 picked for this server), and only for the lineups the sources use, so Jellyfin's guide refreshes never reach Schedules Direct. Update now asks again at most every 15 minutes. Pictures are fetched the first time they're shown, then kept (re-checked now and then: General, below). Remove Jellyfin's own Schedules Direct guide provider once you use this, or it keeps asking them on every refresh.
